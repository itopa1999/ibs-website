School of Virtual Learning

Our Courses
  1. School of Virtual Learning
  2. courses

Learn new ideas, develop new skills, and access our industry experts from anywhere in the world. Our School of Virtual Learning (SVL) programmes are tailored to meet the needs of working professionals around the world.

The vast majority of our online students are working professionals who have demonstrated success in their careers to date. Just as technology is disrupting every aspect of business and society, and learning remains critical in building the capabilities that companies and other organisations need in order to adapt to changes in their environment, so have we embraced the exciting new possibilities created by technology to bring our learning experience and research driven programmes to the comfort and space of professionals and individuals with a flexible approach to learning.

Our online programmes give busy individuals for whom time commitment and rigid scheduling are a continual challenge the flexibility to learn on their own schedule.

Furthermore, organisations can benefit from the scale and speed of online education to give their employees the critical knowledge needed to formulate new strategies and execute on organisational transformation.

Here we take advantage of modern technology and provide online programmes with a perfect blend of research, experiential learning, and thought leadership for individuals who are on the go.

Why School of Virtual Learning

Convenient and friendly

Participants can study at the comfort of their homes, offices, or on the-go using your PC and any Smart Mobile Cost effective: It is affordable (at a subsidised fee), saves the cost of transportation, hotel accommodation, feeding etc. The course are offered at generally affordable rates without compromising standard andquality.

Zero Commutation

This platform allows study without being bothered about the stress of commuting from one geographical location to the other.

Mobile

Significant attraction for all prospective participants in our e- learning programme, is the introduction of learning through mobile Technology (Mobile Phones) whereby, all participants could simultaneously receive lecture broadcasts on their enabled smart mobile phones.

Greater retention and self-paced: Learners can go at their own pace, not at the pace of the slowest member of a group.

Environmental Friendliness

E-learning courses are entirely self-contained, presenting all learning content online and providing content online and providing alternatives to paper-based forms of communication through such tools as email, PDF manuals, and other web-based tools (but the Hard-copy Certificate, upon request).

What programme is for you?

Under our e-Learning platform, we offer 15 wide-ranging courses covering every aspect of capacity needs of a growing economy, from Project Management to Business Management and General Management. Participants have the opportunity of learning from practicing professionals from not less than 5 continents of the World.

Ibadan Business School is committed to learning & development of it’s participants. Through the richly engaging courses available.
