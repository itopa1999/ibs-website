Big Things
Start Small

We help entrepreneurs make a difference. To leverage the benefits of MSMEs in Nigeria, MSMEs need leaders who bring new ideas and are able to manage in a volatile economy. In our commitment to serve micro, small and medium enterprises, the School of Enterprise Management provides hands on, experiential entrepreneurship education and co-created consultancy services that support graduates, prospective and practicing entrepreneurs as well as business professionals in creating value and building sustainable businesses.

Entrepreneurial Programme

We don’t believe entrepreneurs are individuals who are born with a certain set of characteristics. We believe you can learn to act and think entrepreneurially to create the future you want and here are programmes we have designed to help you learn all that you require.
