Why choosing Us

If you are prepared to engage and be engaged, if you deeply care about our nation, continent, your business or future and are committed to helping lead the way forward to a better future, Ibadan Business School presents an opportunity like no other.

At Ibadan Business School we are committed to excellent experiential knowledge facilitation, innovative research, personal and intellectual growth and a conducive working and learning environment, and we are diligent about creating an environment that is both inclusive and diverse. Our staff, faculty and students come from different backgrounds – intellectually, ethnically, economically, and geographically – and we would not have it any other way.

Our Mission

Our mission is to produce thought leading individuals and ideas that influence growth in Africa. To empower and inspire individuals with information, learning, research and co-created ideas they need to change lives, societies, organisations and Africa.

What we Do

At Ibadan Business School, we are committed to providing capacity building services to individuals in the two most important sectors of the economy, the public sector and the micro, small and medium enterprises sector.

Ibadan Business School (IBS) is a learning enterprise established with an overriding vision to bridge, through educational programmes, research and influence, the capacity needs of individuals within two important sectors- the Public Sector and the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises. Ibadan Business School was conceived and designed to provide a customised response to developing nation’s socio-economic development challenges through its tailor-made, unique and home-grown solutions. The School prides itself as the only Business learning enterprise in Nigeria that focuses its service offerings primarily on the two most important sectors of Developing Economies– the Public Sector and the Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises(MSMEs).
