School of Enterprise Management

Our Courses
  1. School of Enterprise Management
  2. courses

We help entrepreneurs make a difference. To leverage the benefits of MSMEs in Nigeria, MSMEs need leaders who bring new ideas and are able to manage in a volatile economy.
In our commitment to serve micro, small and medium enterprises, the School of Enterprise Management provides hands on, experiential entrepreneurship education and co-created consultancy services that support graduates, prospective and practicing entrepreneurs as well as business professionals in creating value and building sustainable businesses.
