School of Development Management

It is an awfully frustrating time in the world. There are many challenging problems and we know it is our job to fix them. Through the School of Development Management, we provide programmes and research designed to equip individuals at all levels of public service with the tools necessary to successfully manage and meet the changing needs of their departments, ministries, constituents and communities.

Training Programmes

Offered at our School of Development Management are executive certificate programmes that have been carefully selected with rich contents designed to run on a highly intensive, hands-on classroom platform as follows:

  1. MEC-Ps—Modular Executive Certificate Programmes
  2. SEC-Ps — Short Executive Certificate Programmes
  3. Online Modular and Short Term Executive Certificate Programmes.
Check our Courses

Consultancy Services

The School of Development Management in addition to being able to conduct research and do training needs analysis is fully equipped to provide on request customised programmes in the following areas of specialisation:

  1. Development Project Management
  2. Finance and Administration
  3. Education and Health Management
  4. ICT General Management
Contact us for more details

In-Plant Training Programmes

With the aid of our well-equipped, compact and mobile training equipment, our training programmes can be delivered in-plant in the premises of our various clients. These programmes are organized at the convenience of our clients in their premises, putting into consideration operational convenience. During training sessions, participants are taken through areas of operations that relate to the subject matter covered during the specific training programme, while giving rooms for participants to ask questions and have answers given to them.
