Youth Entrepreneurial Development Programme (YEDP)

From Ideas and Side Hustles to Sustainable Enterprises

Transform your venture into a market-ready enterprise

The Youth Entrepreneurial Development Programme (YEDP) is a practical online entrepreneurship development and business incubation programme designed by the School of Enterprise Management, Ibadan Business School, to equip young people with the knowledge, skills, systems, and support required to build successful and sustainable businesses.

Do you have a business idea, a side hustle, a skill-based venture, or an existing small business? YEDP provides the practical entrepreneurial training, mentorship, business advisory support, and enterprise development guidance needed to transform your venture into a structured, market-ready, and growth-oriented enterprise.

Why YEDP?

Bridging the gap between raw ideas and structured business success

Nigeria has one of the largest youth populations in Africa, with millions of young people actively engaged in entrepreneurship, self-employment, informal enterprises, digital businesses, vocational services, agriculture, and creative ventures. However, many entrepreneurs struggle with:

  • Lack of business structure
  • Poor financial management
  • Weak marketing strategies
  • Limited business knowledge
  • Regulatory and compliance challenges
  • Inadequate record keeping
  • Limited access to mentorship & business support
YEDP was created to bridge these gaps and empower participants with practical enterprise development skills that support business growth, sustainability, and profitability.

What is YEDP?

YEDP is a 6-month online enterprise development and business incubation programme for young and emerging entrepreneurs aged 18–40 years. The programme combines:

  • Interactive Online Learning (IBS-YEDP LMS)
  • Enterprise Incubation
  • Mentoring
  • Business Advisory Support
  • Business Clinics
  • Practical Assignments
  • Peer Learning
  • Sector-Based Case Studies
  • Digital Enterprise Exposure
  • Business Pitch Presentations

Programme Structure

Basic Level
Duration: 6 Months — Foundational entrepreneurship skills, business idea validation, feasibility analysis, business registration.
Intermediate Level
Duration: 6 Months — Financial management, legal frameworks, marketing systems, staff management, digital tools.
Advanced Level
Duration: 6 Months — Growth strategies, mergers, risk management, succession planning, AI & innovation leadership.

Programme Fee: ₦50,000 per Module (Level)

Each level (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced) is ₦50,000. Pay as you grow.
